A1

UNDERSTAND

Clarify the problem space

The team’s title suggests a possible information-management difficulty. Its causes, impact and specific users still need confirmation.

WORKING HYPOTHESIS · NOT A FINDING

Students may find it difficult to keep track of several kinds of college information.

Test this with students in the selected context before treating it as a fact. Identify which information, tasks and situations matter most to them.

SEARCH FIELD TO CLARIFY

Which student group experiences the issue most? To be determined.

Current practice

Why it matters

A22 · OBSERVATION PLAN

Plan the interaction before the visit

Who
Choose a relevant student group with the guide.
Where / when
Record the real setting and date when the study takes place.
Task
Ask students to walk through a recent information-finding or planning task.
Record
Note actions, sources, obstacles and context. Avoid names or identifying details unless permission is given.

OPEN-ENDED PROMPTS · PROPOSED

Use neutral questions

  • “Can you walk me through the last time you needed one of these details?”
  • “Where did you look first, and what did you do next?”
  • “Was anything confusing or difficult in that situation?”
  • “How did you decide which information to trust?”

These are suggested prompts, not answers or findings. Choose only what fits the approved study.
